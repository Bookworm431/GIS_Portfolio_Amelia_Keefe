## Flooded Homes in the Tampa Bay Area with Respect to Flood Plains

**Event/Course:** GIS 3034C  
**Date:** Fall 2024  
**Co-authors:** None  

### Overview
Conducted a spatial analysis to determine how many homes in the Tampa Bay area were flooded by Hurricane Milton and whether they fell within designated 100-year floodplain zones. Utilized multi-tier spatial overlay tools to quantify property exposure and flood risk.

### Key Methods
- Added floodplain and storm inundation data layers onto a map of the Tampa Bay area.
- Executed the Union geoprocessing tool to merge inundation layers with flood hazard zones.
- Applied Select by Attribute and Clip functions to isolate residential structures within the flooded zones, followed by tabular summarization to calculate flooded houses per floodplain.

### Tools Used
ArcGIS Pro, Union Tool, Select by Attribute, Clip, Spatial Overlay Analysis
