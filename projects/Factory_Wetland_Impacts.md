# Lab #04 - Factory Project Wetland Impacts

**Date:** November 2024  
**Course:** GIS 3043C  
**Tools:** ArcGIS Pro  

## Overview
Conducted a detailed wetland impact analysis for a proposed factory footprint and its surrounding 50-foot work-zone construction buffer. Created a focused, high-impact map utilizing Color Infrared (CIR) aerial imagery and quantitative spatial intersections.

## Methods
* **Data Acquisition & Preparation:** Loaded the project workspace data from `Lab_04.zip`, including the proposed factory boundary, 50-foot buffer, and regional hydrography layers.
* **Spatial Intersection & Impact Quantification:** Analyzed wetland intersections within the 50-foot construction buffer, categorizing impacted areas by FLUCCS code, feature count, and total acreage (including FLUCCS 621 with 1 wetland spanning 1.2524 acres, and FLUCCS 641 with 3 wetlands spanning 1.5674 acres).
* **Symbolization & Cartography:** Symbolized all study area wetlands by `WRAP_RANK` using the standard classification symbology layer. Applied semi-transparent styling to impacted features to ensure underlying DOQQ Color Infrared aerial imagery remained visible.
* **Layout Design & Export:** Integrated a structured text box summarizing impacted wetland acreage by FLUCCS code. Configured standard cartographic elements (neatline, north arrow, scale bar, legend, and name) and exported the final map layout to `Lab_04_Keefe.pdf`.

## Skills Demonstrated
* Spatial Intersection & Buffer Analysis
* Quantitative Impact Assessment & Tabular Data Summarization
* Advanced Cartographic Transparency & CIR Imagery Integration
* Professional Layout Design & Scale Management
