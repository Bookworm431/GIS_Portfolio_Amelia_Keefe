# Practical Exam #02 - Wetland Impact Analysis & Mapping

**Date:** November 2024  
**Course:** GIS 3043C  
**Tools:** ArcGIS Pro  

## Overview
Prepared a professional, landscape-oriented map at a scale of 1” = 500 feet illustrating wetland impacts along the preferred roadway alignment. Focused on applying precise scale management, specialized labeling rules, and transparent impact symbology.

## Methods
* **Data Acquisition & Preparation:** Loaded the `Exam_02.aprx` project workspace, filtering features to display only the preferred roadway alternative centerline and its corresponding right-of-way (ROW).
* **Spatial & Attribute Styling:** Symbolized all study area wetlands to keep underlying aerial imagery visible. Applied distinct semi-transparent fill styling to highlight specific wetland areas impacted by the roadway construction.
* **Custom Labeling:** Configured dual-tier labeling rules across the map: labeled all original wetlands with their respective FLUCCS codes, and labeled each impacted wetland zone directly with its corresponding acreage.
* **Layout Design & Export:** Configured a Letter-size landscape layout at a scale of 1:6,000 (1" = 500 ft). Integrated a clear, descriptive title, properly capitalized legend categories, a scale bar, north arrow, neatline, and student name label before exporting to a high-resolution PDF (`Exam_02_Keefe.pdf`).

## Skills Demonstrated
* Precise Map Scale Management (1" = 500 ft / 1:6,000)
* Multi-Tier Attribute Labeling (FLUCCS Codes & Impact Acreage)
* Transparent Overlay Symbology for Wetland Impact Assessment
* Cartographic Layout Design & Professional Standardization
