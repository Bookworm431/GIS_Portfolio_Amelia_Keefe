# Lab #4: GIS Applications for Social Planning (Environmental Justice)

**Date:** March 2025  
**Course:** SYA 4354
**Tools:** ArcGIS Pro

## Overview
Evaluated the spatial relationship between industrial facilities and urban populations in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, by analyzing pollution exposure and demographic distribution. Created a professional map layout featuring a 2-mile industrial buffer, city boundary clipping, and custom cartographic symbology.

## Methods
- **Data Acquisition & Preparation:** Imported Pittsburgh city limits, industrial facility data, and regional poverty/demographic data layers into ArcGIS Pro.
- **Buffer & Overlay Analysis:** Generated a 2-mile buffer around industrial sites to identify surrounding communities and evaluate exposure zones.
- **Cartographic Refinement:** Applied a custom city-limit clip to streamline the study area and prioritized the local river hydrography as the highest visual layer for geographic context. Customized map symbology to reflect regional team colors.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Environmental Justice & Proximity Analysis
- Buffer Creation & Multi-Layer Clipping
- Cartographic Visual Hierarchy & Basemap Management

## Outputs
![Pittsburgh Environmental Justice Map](../images/Lab_04_Pittsburgh_Map.png)

## Challenges & Solutions
- **Challenge:** Managing visual clutter when overlapping complex industrial buffer zones with dense urban demographic layers.  
- **Solution:** Utilized a precise city-limit clip and adjusted layer ordering to keep hydrography and boundaries clearly legible.

- Data Sources: EPA Toxic Release Inventory (TRI), Census Bureau Demographics, OpenStreetMap
